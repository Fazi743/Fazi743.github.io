## Welcome!

I am **Syed Faizan Hussain Shah**, a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at **Jeju National University, South Korea**.

My research focuses on developing **efficient, scalable, and resource-aware Artificial Intelligence systems**, with particular interest in:

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- Model Quantization
- Lightweight Fine-Tuning
- Efficient Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Intelligent Robotics

My goal is to bridge cutting-edge AI research with practical real-world deployment by developing efficient foundation models that can operate effectively in resource-constrained environments.

Thank you for visiting my website!
