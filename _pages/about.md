## Hello, I'm Syed Faizan Hussain Shah 👋

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at **Jeju National University, South Korea**, where I conduct research in the **Data-Centric Generative AI Lab (DCAL)**.

My research focuses on developing **efficient and scalable Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** through model quantization, lightweight fine-tuning, and parameter-efficient learning techniques. My goal is to make advanced AI systems more practical for deployment on resource-constrained devices while maintaining high performance.

Before joining Jeju National University, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from **COMSATS University Islamabad**. During my research career, I have also worked on intelligent railway infrastructure monitoring, applying computer vision and deep learning techniques to high-speed railway inspection and maintenance systems.

My broader research interests include Efficient AI, Large Language Models, Vision-Language Models, Computer Vision, Multimodal Learning, Edge AI, Robotics, and Intelligent Transportation Systems.

Beyond academic research, I collaborate with researchers and industry clients by developing AI solutions, creating scientific visualizations, implementing deep learning models, and supporting publication-quality research. Through my freelance work, I have successfully completed more than **40 AI and academic projects** for researchers and organizations worldwide.

My long-term vision is to contribute to the next generation of efficient foundation models that are scalable, resource-aware, and capable of solving real-world challenges across science, engineering, and society.
