I am particularly interested in understanding how model optimization, lightweight adaptation, and language representation influence the behavior and performance of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs). Through empirical investigation and systematic experimentation, I aim to bridge the gap between advances in foundation models and their deployment under real-world computational constraints. My broader interests include multilingual AI, trustworthy machine learning, multimodal intelligence, and resource-aware AI systems.

## Research Approach

My research combines empirical investigation, controlled experimentation, and quantitative evaluation. Depending on the problem, I emphasize four complementary dimensions:

<div class="rgrid rgrid--approach">

<div class="acard">
  <div class="acard-icon">📈</div>
  <h3 class="acard-title">Model Quality</h3>
  <p>Measuring task performance and identifying degradation introduced by optimization, quantization, or changes in input representation.</p>
</div>

<div class="acard">
  <div class="acard-icon">⚡</div>
  <h3 class="acard-title">Computational Efficiency</h3>
  <p>Evaluating model size, memory consumption, inference latency, throughput, and computational requirements.</p>
</div>

<div class="acard">
  <div class="acard-icon">🌍</div>
  <h3 class="acard-title">Cross-Lingual Robustness</h3>
  <p>Comparing model behavior across languages, scripts, and alternative representations of the same meaning.</p>
</div>

<div class="acard">
  <div class="acard-icon">🔁</div>
  <h3 class="acard-title">Reproducibility</h3>
  <p>Controlled settings, appropriate baselines, ablation studies, and systematic evaluation to ensure reliable conclusions.</p>
</div>
