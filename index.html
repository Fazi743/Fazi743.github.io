Syed Faizan Hussain Shah

Master's Student in Artificial Intelligence
Jeju National University, South Korea

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Jeju National University, South Korea, working on efficient, multilingual, and reliable AI systems. My research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), with an emphasis on model quantization, compression, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, efficient inference, and multilingual evaluation.

I am particularly interested in understanding how foundation models can become more efficient, robust, and accessible across languages and real-world environments. I aim to pursue PhD research in trustworthy and efficient AI while also exploring research-oriented AI/ML engineering and applied scientist roles in industry.
