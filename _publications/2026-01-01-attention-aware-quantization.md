## Abstract

This paper proposes an Attention-Aware Adaptive Block-Wise Quantization framework combined with Rank-Precision Co-Designed qLoRA+ for efficient fine-tuning of Large Language Models. The method improves memory efficiency while maintaining strong downstream performance through adaptive precision allocation and parameter-efficient adaptation.
